# Brand tone and production rules

## Voice

Practical, specific, warm, and direct. Prefer one useful idea over a list of vague hacks. Never use fake scarcity, shame, invented customer results, or unsupported statistics.

## Short-form scripts

The hook should create an honest curiosity gap in at most eight words. The body should resolve that gap with a practical framework or example. The CTA should be proportionate: save, follow, review a guide, or ask a relevant question.

## Visuals and narration

Use visual metaphors and original B-roll rather than unauthorized trademarks or real-person likenesses. Every voice or image reference requires ownership or explicit permission. Review pronunciation and disclose synthetic media where a platform or jurisdiction requires it.

## Publishing

A human reviews claims, captions, translations, audio, visuals, rights, and disclosure before manually uploading. No part of this studio has permission to post externally.
