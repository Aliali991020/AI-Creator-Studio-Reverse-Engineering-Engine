# Studio offer guide

> Example knowledge document. Replace these offers and prices before using Maya with real inquiries. Maya must answer from this guide and must never invent checkout or booking actions.

## Creator Systems Course

A self-paced course for creators who want a repeatable research, scripting, production, and review workflow. Includes lesson videos, workflow templates, and caption review checklists.

- Example price: $79 USD
- Best fit: learners who want structured, independent study
- Not included: individualized account audits or guaranteed platform growth
- Checkout state: Maya may log checkout intent; a human must provide the official checkout route

## 1:1 Creator Workflow Coaching

A focused coaching engagement for a creator who wants direct feedback on one production workflow. Includes one 60-minute call and a written action summary.

- Example price: $250 USD per session
- Best fit: a specific workflow obstacle and a desired one-month outcome
- Not included: done-for-you account management or guaranteed revenue
- Booking state: Maya may log a booking request, but cannot create an external calendar event

## Brand collaborations

Custom-scoped sponsored content or user-generated content for a relevant brand. Qualification should establish campaign outcome, audience fit, deliverables, usage rights, exclusivity, timeline, and budget.

- Pricing: custom after scope is confirmed; do not quote a price without a written scope
- Required review: claims, disclosures, usage rights, and final creative approval
- Maya never accepts a contract or payment automatically
